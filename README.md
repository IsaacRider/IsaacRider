## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science** graduate from the **University of Missouri** (minor in Mathematics) with a passion for coding and problem-solving. I have a strong foundation in **Java, Python, C, and C++**, along with hands-on experience in **SQL, React, Electron, REST APIs, GitHub,** and **Linux environments**.

I cofounded **Tonetta**, where I architected a cross-platform desktop application with AI-driven features and led a team of engineering interns. I've also built enterprise web applications as an **Applications Development Intern** at the University of Missouri.

I enjoy collaborating on meaningful projects, learning new technologies, and continuously growing as a developer. I'm eager to take on new challenges and contribute to impactful, innovative work.
