## 👨‍💻 Software Developer

Hello! I'm **Isaac Rider**, a **Computer Science** graduate from the **University of Missouri** (minor in Mathematics) with a passion for coding and problem-solving.

I have a strong foundation in **Java, Python, C, and C++**, along with hands-on experience in **SQL**, **React**, **Electron**, **REST APIs**, **GitHub**, and **Linux environments**. I cofounded **Tonetta**, where I architected a cross-platform desktop application with AI-driven features and led a team of engineering interns, and I've built enterprise web applications as an Applications Development Intern at the University of Missouri.

I enjoy collaborating on meaningful projects, learning new technologies, and continuously growing as a developer. I'm eager to take on new challenges and contribute to impactful and innovative work.
